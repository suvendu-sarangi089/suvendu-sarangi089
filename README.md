## `who am i`

I’m Suvendu Sarangi, a passionate and detail-oriented fresher in Data Science and Data Analytics with strong proficiency in SQL, PostgreSQL, Excel, Power BI, and Python.

💡 I enjoy working with data to clean, analyze, and transform raw information into meaningful insights that support better decision-making.

📊 I have hands-on experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), ETL processes, dashboard creation, and business intelligence reporting through academic and personal projects.
